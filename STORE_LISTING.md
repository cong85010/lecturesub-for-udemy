LectureSub for Udemy mang đến trải nghiệm học tập đỉnh cao trên Udemy: xem phụ đề song ngữ song song, tra cứu nhanh nghĩa từ vựng chuyên ngành ngay trên video và lưu sổ từ vựng để ôn tập bất cứ lúc nào.

2. Tra từ tức thì & Tự động tạm dừng (Contextual Dictionary)

### Extension Name
```text
LectureSub for Udemy — Bilingual Subtitles & Vocabulary
```

### Short Name
```text
LectureSub
```

### Summary / Short Description (tối đa 132 ký tự)
```text
Smart bilingual subtitles, instant word lookup on video, and a local vocabulary notebook for Udemy lectures. 100% free, no API key.
```

### Detailed Description (English)
```text
LectureSub for Udemy enhances your learning experience on Udemy: watch courses with simultaneous bilingual subtitles, inspect technical terminology definitions directly on the video, and save words to your local notebook for seamless review.

100% Free, privacy-first, no account required, and no external API keys needed.

✦ KEY FEATURES

1. Smart Bilingual Subtitles
- Displays simultaneous dual subtitle lines (original course language and translated captions) synchronized with the lecture video.
- Toggle between dual subtitles, source-only, or translation-only mode with 1 click.
- Automatically detects and adapts to Udemy video players.

2. Instant Word Lookup & Auto-Pause (Contextual Dictionary)
- Click on any word in the subtitle line to inspect its definition directly on the video screen.
- Automatically pauses the video when inspecting definitions and resumes playback when you close the card (fully customizable).

3. Personal Vocabulary Notebook
- Save difficult technical terms with original sentence context and timestamp in just one click.
- Smart diacritic-free search to find any saved term quickly.
- One-click CSV export ready for Anki, Excel, or Google Sheets.

4. "Reading Room" Aesthetics & Customization
- Minimalist, high-contrast, eye-friendly design tailored for deep focus and late-night learning sessions.
- Easily adjust font size, color, typography, background opacity, line height, and subtitle placement.

✦ PRIVACY & SECURITY
- Zero personal data, credentials, or browsing history collection.
- All caption settings and vocabulary records are stored locally on your device (Local Storage).
- Uses Google Chrome's built-in on-device Translator API without transmitting course content to third-party servers.

✦ GETTING STARTED
1. Open any Udemy course video and enable subtitles in Udemy's CC player menu.
2. Select the matching source language in LectureSub.
3. Click "Enable Free Translation" on the video overlay to activate.

---
Disclaimer:
LectureSub for Udemy is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, sponsored, or endorsed by Udemy, Inc. "Udemy" is a registered trademark of Udemy, Inc.
```
